# Rampart × Lifto — seven-day campaign runbook

**Status:** preparation draft. Do not start the countdown until the pre-launch gates
below are complete and the fundraise date is confirmed with Lifto.

This runbook follows the founder-provided Lifto 7-Day Plan dated 16 November 2026.
Its point targets are campaign goals, not guaranteed leaderboard outcomes.

## Campaign scoreboard

The Lifto plan's stated scoring model:
- Community vote: 10 points; target 50 votes.
- Follow: 5 points; target 50 follows.
- Community X post tagging `@userampart`: 20 points; target 14 posts.
- Founder X post tagging `@LiftoWorld`: 10 points; target 11 posts.
- Founder Lifto update: 10 points; target one per day.
- `@userampart` follows `@LiftoWorld`: 5 points.

The plan estimates approximately 1,215 points from the full activity plan, plus
the stated starting score. The leaderboard is not a fundraising outcome or
validation of product-market fit.

Rules from the Lifto plan:
- One wallet, one vote.
- Never buy votes, use bots, or offer tokens, allowlist spots or early access in
  exchange for votes.
- Only ask people who have not already voted.
- Get permission before using a builder's name, logo, quote or testimonial.
- Do not publish unverified numbers or claims.

Do not start Day 1 until these are true:

- [ ] Confirm the fundraiser opening date, page URL, instrument and terms with Lifto.
- [ ] Complete the Lifto page: product description, logo, live demo, transaction
      evidence, technical paper, GitHub and team/reviewer information where real.
- [ ] Retake the Lifto AI assessment after the actual page and documentation fixes.
- [ ] Verify every public technical claim against the exact deployed program ID.
- [ ] Include the current Pyth decoder limitation and source-only status in the paper.
- [ ] Record a two-minute walkthrough for Lifto to edit into the Day 4 product video.
- [ ] Prepare a 60–90 second founder video using the real founder story.
- [ ] Finish the “Why Rampart, why now” article.
- [ ] Confirm the $100 weekly Lifto writer pool, eligibility requirements, dates
      and claim process. Do not promise the pool until confirmed.
- [ ] If running a separate Rampart community giveaway, agree the prize, judging
      rules and terms with Lifto before announcing it.
- [ ] Confirm which people have actually tested Rampart and obtain permission for
      any public quotes.
- [ ] Prepare the founder's pinned “back us” instructions with the real page URL.

## Posting cadence

The Lifto plan specifies 14:00 UTC each day. Confirm the date and local conversion
before scheduling. Every day includes:
1. One or more X posts from the founder/product account.
2. One Lifto founder update.
3. Direct community engagement and thank-yous.

## Content guardrails

### Safe to say now
- Rampart is an on-chain policy vault for trading agents on Solana.
- The public Devnet demo is available at https://rampart.pxxl.click.
- The founder reports the production API and live demo have been tested.
- A founder-provided Devnet transaction can be inspected here:
  https://explorer.solana.com/tx/5m4AwxoMbioSWHKVvpuEhQfRy6Ch9QpLoyrEuEL3Rx63bfCx2zJ8dqc2L5uNia4uKyq9Bf9JEuhCtueq6zAdr9MR?cluster=devnet
- The current deployment is Devnet and immutable.
- The deployed Pyth decoder has a known field-offset defect; corrected source
  safeguards require a new program ID and deployment.
- The Meteora DBC guard is source/CI only; Rampart does not execute swaps.
- No mainnet, external audit, customer, TVL or revenue claim is currently
  documented in the repository.

### Do not say until evidence exists
- “37 tests passing” unless the current CI run is still green and the count is
  rechecked.
- “8 actions, 7 refused” unless the current production run's actual step log and
  signatures support that exact count.
- “Pyth price band / halted-feed protection is live.”
- “Meteora swaps,” “live DBC guard,” “mainnet ready,” “audited,” or “battle-tested.”
- “Users,” “partners,” “design partners,” “revenue,” “TVL,” or “integrations” without
  evidence and permission.
- Any raise amount, token allocation, valuation, return, or use-of-funds percentage
  that the founder has not approved.

## Post production checklist

For every post, record:
- final copy and asset;
- account and publication time;
- Lifto tag and correct page link;
- source for each number or technical claim;
- URL and engagement after publication;
- replies/questions that need founder follow-up.

## Founder inputs required before final copy

1. The real event or observation that made you start Rampart.
2. The amount being raised, currency, instrument and what the round unlocks.
3. The exact Lifto fundraiser page URL and confirmed opening date.
4. Whether the Lifto writer pool is confirmed for this campaign.
5. Any real builders who tested the demo and explicitly permit a quote or name.
6. The founder's preferred four-week delivery commitments and budget allocation.

Until those inputs are confirmed, campaign content should remain in draft and no
countdown should be posted.
